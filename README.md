# Don't Starve Together Mods

A collection of mods I've developed for **Don't Starve Together (DST)**, focused on improving gameplay, adding useful mechanics, and enhancing quality of life.

## Mods

### 1. Ice Flingomatic Overhaul
A complete overhaul of the Ice Flingomatic, featuring customizable range, infinite fuel, faster snowballs, and improved fire suppression. Also introduces craftable markers that allow Flingomatics to automatically target specific locations.

### 2. Loot Stash & Antler Detectors
Adds two craftable tracking devices to make winter exploration easier:
- **Loot Stash Detector:** Locates Klaus's Loot Stash.
- **Antler Detector:** Locates the nearest antlered No-Eyed Deer.

Both devices have 10 uses and display directional markers to help locate their targets.

### 3. MyDock
Introduces expandable rope docks that let players build walkable pathways across the ocean. Docks can extend or branch in different directions, and a special Dock Hammer allows players to dismantle and recover dock pieces.
