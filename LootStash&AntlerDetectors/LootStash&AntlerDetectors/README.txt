Loot Stash & Antler Detectors v1.0.0
Author: Creeirdo

FEATURES
- Loot Stash Detector: finds Klaus's Loot Stash.
- Antler Detector: finds the nearest antlered No-Eyed Deer.
- Both detectors have 10 uses.
- Direction markers last 20 seconds.
- Both require an Alchemy Engine.

RECIPES
Loot Stash Detector: 4 Cut Stone + 1 Red Gem + 1 Gears
Antler Detector: 4 Cut Stone + 1 Blue Gem + 1 Gears

INSTALLATION
Place the WinterDetectors folder in Don't Starve Together/mods and enable it for the world.

WORKSHOP
The in-game mod icon is configured through images/modicon.tex and images/modicon.xml.
